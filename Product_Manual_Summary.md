# HP Laser 100 Series Printer User Guide: Product Manual Summary

**Target Models:** HP Laser 103 Series, HP Laser 107 Series, HP Laser 108 Series  
**Document Class:** Executive Product Manual & Technical Summary  

---

## 1. Product Overview & Key Benefits

The **HP Laser 100 Series** (comprising models HP Laser 103, 107, and 108) is a compact, high-efficiency monochrome laser printing system designed for personal offices, small business environments, and home workstations requiring fast, reliable monochrome document output.

### Key Product Features & Benefits
* **High-Speed Printing**: Delivers fast monochrome laser print speeds up to 20 to 21 pages per minute (PPM).
* **Compact Footprint**: Ergonomic chassis design suited for space-constrained desks and workstations.
* **Eco-Conscious Power Management**:
  * **Auto-Off / Power Save Mode**: Substantially reduces power consumption when the device is idle.
  * **N-up Printing**: Supports printing multiple pages onto a single sheet of paper to reduce media usage.
  * **Recycled Paper Compatibility**: Fully optimized for recycled paper stock without sacrificing print quality.
* **Flexible Connectivity Options**:
  * **USB 2.0 High-Speed Connection**: Direct desktop connectivity.
  * **Wireless (Wi-Fi 802.11b/g/n)**: Wireless networking available on select models (107w / 108w).
  * **Wi-Fi Direct & Mobile Printing**: Direct wireless printing from smartphones and tablets via Apple AirPrint and the HP Smart Application.

## 2. Important Safety Precautions & Information

Before operating or servicing the printer, review and follow all safety recommendations to prevent personal injury, fire risks, or equipment damage.

### Operational Safety Guidelines
* **Electrical Safety**:
  * Connect the power cable exclusively to a grounded wall AC power outlet.
  * Never use damaged power cords or overloaded multi-outlet extension strips.
  * Turn off the printer and disconnect the power cord before internal cleaning or inspection.
* **Thermal Precautions (Fuser Area)**:
  * **High Temperature Warning**: The internal fuser area operates at high temperatures during printing. Allow the unit to cool down before opening the top cover or clearing internal paper jams.
* **Toner & Chemical Safety**:
  * Keep toner cartridges out of reach of children.
  * Avoid inhaling toner dust or allowing loose toner to come into contact with skin or eyes. Wash exposed skin with cold water immediately if contact occurs (hot water causes toner to set into fabric/skin).
  * Never incinerate used toner cartridges, as loose toner dust may ignite and cause fire hazards.
* **Location & Environment Placement**:
  * Place the printer on a stable, flat, non-tilted, non-vibrating surface capable of supporting its full weight.
  * Maintain at least 10–15 cm (4–6 inches) of free clearance around all ventilation slots to prevent internal overheating.
  * Keep away from direct sunlight, moisture, excessive dust, heat sources, and chemical vapors.

## 3. Hardware & Software Installation & Setup

### 3.1 Initial Unboxing & Hardware Setup
1. **Unpacking**: Remove all shipping tape, external protective foam, and internal packing clips from the printer chassis and paper input tray.
2. **Toner Cartridge Preparation**:
   * Open the top output cover.
   * Remove the pre-installed toner cartridge.
   * Gently rock the toner cartridge side-to-side 5 to 6 times to distribute toner evenly.
   * Insert the cartridge securely back into the printer slot until it clicks into place, then close the top cover.
3. **Connecting Power**:
   * Connect the power cord to the rear AC inlet of the printer and plug the other end into an electrical outlet.
   * Press the **Power** button on the control panel to turn on the machine.

### 3.2 Software & Driver Installation
* **Windows Setup (Windows 7 / 8 / 10 / 11)**:
  * Download the HP Laser 100 series printer driver suite from `www.hp.com/support/laser100`.
  * Run the installer executable and follow the on-screen prompt choices (USB Connection or Wireless Setup).
  * Do not connect the USB cable until prompted by the installer program.
* **Mac OS & AirPrint Setup**:
  * AirPrint requires no additional driver installation on macOS OS X 10.7 Lion or newer.
  * Add the printer directly through **System Preferences > Printers & Scanners**.

### 3.3 Wireless & Mobile Setup (Wi-Fi Models)
* **Wi-Fi Protected Setup (WPS)**:
  * Press and hold the **WPS** button on the printer control panel for approximately 10 seconds until the Wi-Fi LED blinks.
  * Within 2 minutes, press the **WPS** button on your wireless network router. The LED illuminates solid when connected.
* **Wi-Fi Direct Printing**:
  * Enable Wi-Fi Direct via the configuration sheet to allow mobile devices to connect directly to the printer's SSID without a local router.

## 4. Media & Paper Handling Specifications

Proper paper selection and loading ensure consistent print performance and prevent paper jam incidents.

### 4.1 Supported Media Sizes & Types
* **Plain Paper**: Letter, Legal, Executive, A4, A5, B5 (JIS), Oficio.
* **Specialty Media**: Envelopes (Com10, DL, C5, Monarch), Labels, Cardstock, Recycled, Preprinted, Bond, Archive paper.
* **Media Weight Ratings**:
  * **Standard Tray**: 60 to 120 g/m² (16 to 32 lb bond).
  * **Labels & Heavyweight Media**: Up to 163 g/m² (43 lb bond).

### 4.2 Paper Tray Capacity & Loading Instructions
* **Main Input Tray Capacity**: Up to 150 sheets of 80 g/m² plain paper.
* **Output Tray Capacity**: Up to 100 sheets face-down.
* **Loading Procedure**:
  1. Pull out the main input tray.
  2. Adjust the paper width and length guides outward.
  3. Flex and fan the paper stack to eliminate static cling, then align the edges.
  4. Insert paper print-side up into the tray.
  5. Slide the paper guides inward until they rest against the edges of the paper stack without bending the paper.

## 5. Device Control Panel & Basic Operations

### 5.1 Control Panel Overview
The control panel features intuitive LED status indicators and push-buttons for straightforward operation:

| Button / LED Component | Primary Function & Behavior |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Power LED / Button** | Powers the unit ON or OFF. Blinks when printing or entering Power Save mode. |
| **Resume / Cancel Button** | Resumes paused print jobs, cancels active print commands, or prints configuration sheets. |
| **Wireless Button / LED** | Turns Wi-Fi ON/OFF, initiates WPS setup, and displays wireless connection status. |
| **Toner LED Indicator** | Indicates toner status (Solid Green = Normal, Blinking Orange = Low, Solid Orange = Very Low/Empty). |
| **Error LED Indicator** | Illuminates orange when a paper jam occurs, paper tray is empty, or cover is open. |

### 5.2 Printing Information & Diagnostic Reports
* **Configuration Report**: Press and hold the **Resume/Cancel** button for **10 seconds** until the Power LED blinks slowly, then release.
* **Supplies Information Report**: Press and hold the **Resume/Cancel** button for **15 seconds** until the Power LED blinks rapidly, then release.

## 6. Maintenance & Component Care

Regular maintenance prolongs printer life and ensures high-quality document output.

### 6.1 Replacing the Toner Cartridge
1. Turn off the printer power switch and open the top output cover.
2. Grasp the handle of the depleted toner cartridge and pull it upward and outward to remove it.
3. Remove the new genuine HP replacement toner cartridge from its protective bag.
4. Gently rock the cartridge side-to-side 5 to 6 times to loosen internal toner powder.
5. Remove all protective tape and covers from the new cartridge.
6. Slide the new cartridge firmly into the slot until seated, then close the top cover.

### 6.2 Cleaning the Device
* **Exterior Cleaning**: Clean the outer casing using a soft cloth slightly moistened with clean water. Never use solvent cleaners containing ammonia or alcohol.
* **Interior Cleaning**:
  1. Unplug the power cable and allow the fuser area to cool down completely.
  2. Open the top cover and remove the toner cartridge.
  3. Wipe away loose toner and paper dust from the cartridge cavity and paper path using a dry, lint-free cloth.
  4. Reinstall the toner cartridge and close the top cover securely.

## 7. Troubleshooting Guide & Error Resolutions

When operational errors occur, consult the diagnostic table below for corrective procedures:

### 7.1 Error Diagnostics & Resolutions

| Issue / Error Symptom | Cause | Recommended Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper Jam in Input Tray** | Misaligned paper or overloaded tray. | Pull jammed paper out slowly. Reload paper below maximum fill capacity. |
| **Paper Jam Inside Fuser** | High temperature area restriction. | Wait for fuser to cool. Open top cover, remove toner cartridge, and pull paper out gently. |
| **Low Toner / Faded Print** | Low toner level in cartridge. | Remove toner cartridge, shake side-to-side to temporarily restore quality, then replace. |
| **Printer Offline / Wi-Fi Drop** | Network disconnection or IP change. | Restart router and printer. Re-verify SSID and print a Network Configuration Sheet. |
| **Multiple Sheet Feed** | Static cling or damaged paper edges. | Fan the paper stack prior to loading. Ensure paper weights match specifications. |
